\begin{abstract}
High-volume GNN prediction APIs serve softmax scores for ranking, routing, A/B tests, and compliance pipelines on graphs from thousands to millions of nodes; production stacks cache by request hash and re-query the same entities under continuous traffic.
Operators sometimes also need a positive fraction of \emph{raw} model posteriors---unmodified $\hat p_v$ for calibration and downstream score consumers---which is stricter than replaying a previously sampled noisy response from a cache or ledger.
Fresh uniform release noise cannot meet that raw-exact SLA; global caches and seeded noise raise ReplayFrac without restoring ExactFrac$\equiv$RawExactFrac.
\textbf{HARP} is a Big-Data score-serving layer for this constrained setting: constructors rank nodes, hop expansion forms a protected set, a protector runs only there, and validation-time Frac search targets Acc under ExactFrac$\ge c$ (study default $c{=}0.60$) with an optional audit dial $\tau$.
On defense-aware LiRA ($n_{\mathrm{shadows}}{=}16$), HARP is feasible for $c{>}0$ with competitive Acc/ECE on large-graph serving probes; residual clean-slice risk is reported as a structural limit of ExactFrac$>0$.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
big data prediction APIs, graph neural networks, response caching, ExactFrac, selective score serving, membership audit
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Motivation}
GNN prediction APIs return softmax vectors for triage, ranking, and analytics at Big-Data scale~\cite{kipf2017gcn,hamilton2017graphsage,hu2020ogb}: graphs with $10^3$--$10^6+$ nodes, high query velocity (clients re-query the same entities), and veracity requirements for downstream score consumers.
Production serving stacks hash requests and cache responses to cut latency under repeated queries~\cite{crankshaw2017clipper,triton2024cache}.
Released scores also leak training membership~\cite{shokri2017membership,salem2019mlleaks,carlini2022membership,olatunji2021mia}.
This paper is about the \emph{systems} intersection of those facts: how to release scores under a raw-posterior service contract while still spending noise where an audit requires it---not a new membership-inference theory paper.

\paragraph{Why ExactFrac is operationally necessary.}
Two contracts are easy to confuse.
\emph{ReplayFrac} asks whether two queries return the same vector (a cache hit or sticky/seeded draw can satisfy this with a \emph{noisy} first answer).
\emph{ExactFrac}$\equiv$RawExactFrac asks whether the released vector equals the unmodified model posterior $\hat p_v$.
Raw equality matters when downstream systems treat API scores as model truth: temperature calibration, threshold routers that must match offline evaluation, A/B analytics that join on score bits, and compliance pipelines that attest ``this is the model's output.''
Caching a noisy draw restores ReplayFrac and QPS; it does \emph{not} restore ExactFrac.
We therefore study
\[
\max_{\Pi}\;\mathrm{Acc}(\Pi)
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\mathrm{ExactFrac}(\Pi)\ge c,
\quad
\mathrm{LiRA}(\Pi)\le\tau
\]
(optional $\tau$), with study default $c{=}0.60$ as an operator dial (Fig.~\ref{fig:exactfrac-pareto}).
Fresh uniform LBP has both fractions ${\approx}0$ (Remark~\ref{rem:uniform-infeasible}).
Table~\ref{tab:sla} shows global-cache and seeded LBP achieve ReplayFrac${=}1$ with ExactFrac${=}0$; selective release (HARP) meets $c{>}0$ without a ledger of non-raw scores.

\paragraph{How to read Acc / ExactFrac / LiRA.}
\textbf{Acc} is task utility (primary objective under the SLA).
\textbf{ExactFrac} is the service constraint: fraction of nodes released as raw $\hat p_v$.
\textbf{LiRA} is an optional audit dial---used to decide how much Frac to spend, not the headline win condition.
In practice: if ExactFrac$\ge c{>}0$ is required, discard rows with ExactFrac${=}0$ even if their LiRA looks better; if only Acc s.t.\ LiRA$\le\tau$ matters, prefer equal-mass LBP and ignore HARP.
Our running Cora example ($c{=}0.60$, $n_{\mathrm{sh}}{=}16$): equal-mass LBP Acc${\approx}0.87$/LiRA${\approx}0.59$/ExactFrac${=}0$ vs.\ HARP Acc${\approx}0.83$/LiRA${\approx}0.67$/ExactFrac${=}0.60$ with much lower clean-majority ECE.

\paragraph{Framework.}
\textbf{HARP} is a constrained score-serving layer for GNN APIs:
(1)~a \emph{constructor} ranks nodes (topology, degree, train-neighbor exposure, confidence/entropy, random, or audit-guided vulnerability);
(2)~hop expansion $\mathcal{P}_k=\mathrm{Ball}_k(\mathcal{R})$ plus a \emph{protector} only on $\mathcal{P}_k$;
(3)~operator dials Frac/Mass/session budget $B$, with \textbf{CFS} searching Frac under $(c,\tau)$.
Default: \emph{release-only} selective Laplace at Frac${=}0.40$ (ExactFrac${=}0.60$).

\paragraph{Headline evidence.}
At $n_{\mathrm{shadows}}{=}16$, HARP meets ExactFrac$\ge 0.60$ while uniform and stateful-noisy LBP cannot; equal-mass LBP remains the right comparator when $c{=}0$ is allowed.
Within the constrained class, constructors cut clean-slice AUROC ($0.669\rightarrow 0.594$), CFS selects Frac under operator $(c,\tau)$, and HARP$\circ$GAP preserves GAP's $\varepsilon$ for public-metadata constructors.
ogbn-arxiv/products probes show Acc and serving cost at volume; privacy audits at that scale are scoped and canary-stressed (not claimed as full-graph LiRA).

\subsection{Gap}
Prior release defenses (LBP~\cite{olatunji2021mia}, MemGuard~\cite{jia2019memguard}) perturb every response and therefore yield ExactFrac${=}0$ unless wrapped in a response ledger that stores non-raw scores.
Train-time GNN MIA methods (GTD, MaskArmor)~\cite{gtd2024,maskarmor2024} and DP-GNNs~\cite{sajadmanesh2023gap,napgnn2023,abadi2016deepdp} do not expose ExactFrac/Mass serving accounting for prediction APIs.
None formalize Acc under ExactFrac$\ge c{>}0$ as a Big-Data score-serving objective with constructors, validation-time Frac search, and trainer composition.

\subsection{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{ExactFrac-constrained API release.} Separate RawExactFrac from ReplayFrac for prediction serving; show fresh and stateful-noisy uniform policies fail ExactFrac$\ge c{>}0$; hop coverage and DP post-processing composition (Prop.~\ref{thm:compose}).
  \item \textbf{HARP serving layer.} Selective, hop-aware release with constructors, validation-time CFS, optional DCS, and HARP$\circ$GAP for finite $\varepsilon$ plus ExactFrac controls under continuous query traffic.
  \item \textbf{Constraint-aware Big-Data evaluation.} Headline LiRA at $n_{\mathrm{sh}}{=}16$; RawExactFrac-vs-ReplayFrac SLA (Table~\ref{tab:sla}); cache microbenchmarks; scoped OGB Acc/serving and canary-stressed probes; clean-slice risk as a structural limit.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
Shadow/confidence MIAs~\cite{shokri2017membership,salem2019mlleaks,yeom2018privacy} and LiRA~\cite{carlini2022membership} are the audit standard for prediction APIs; overconfidence is a recurring cue~\cite{guo2017calibration}.
On graphs, Olatunji et al.~\cite{olatunji2021mia} introduce node-level MIAs and LBP; He et al.~\cite{he2021nodemia}, GTD~\cite{gtd2024}, MaskArmor~\cite{maskarmor2024}, and surveys~\cite{survey2023gnnprivacy} study related attacks/defenses.
MemGuard~\cite{jia2019memguard} adversarially perturbs every confidence vector (ExactFrac${=}0$).
DP-GNNs such as GAP~\cite{sajadmanesh2023gap,abadi2016deepdp} provide train-time $(\varepsilon,\delta)$; structure modulates leakage~\cite{zhu2020beyondhomophily,khosla2026structure}.
\textbf{Positioning:} This is a prediction-API / score-serving paper with an optional membership audit dial.
LBP/MemGuard spend on every node (ExactFrac${=}0$ unless a non-raw ledger is added); GTD/MaskArmor harden training without ExactFrac accounting; GAP answers formal $\varepsilon$ (ExactFrac${=}1$ at eval for train-only noise); HARP answers ExactFrac$\ge c$ serving under continuous query traffic and composes with DP trainers under Prop.~\ref{thm:compose}.
Our GAP-agg and MemGuard baselines are faithful reimplementations, not line-by-line ports of the official codebases---we label them as such throughout.
Primary comparisons under $c{>}0$ are selective policies at matched Frac; equal-mass LBP is the unconstrained ($c{=}0$) reference.

\section{Problem Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Notation}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be an undirected graph with $n=|V|$ nodes, feature matrix $X\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times d}$, and labels $y\in\{0,\ldots,C-1\}^n$.
A random split partitions $V$ into supervised train $\mathcal{T}$, validation $\mathcal{V}$, and test $\mathcal{Q}$ with ratios $40\%/20\%/40\%$ (resampled per seed).
Training is \emph{transductive}: every node participates in message passing; only $\mathcal{T}$ enters the cross-entropy loss.
We write $\hat p_v$ for the softmax posterior at $v$, $r_v$ for a risk score in $[0,1]$, $\mathcal{P}_k$ for the hop-expanded protected set, Mass$=\sum_v\sigma_v$, and Frac$=|\mathcal{P}_k|/n$.

\subsection{Constrained score release}
Operators rarely optimize Acc alone.
They may need usable accuracy \emph{and} a positive fraction of unmodified model posteriors, subject to a membership audit.
\begin{definition}[Constrained Score Release]
Given a trained GNN and release channel, choose a release policy $\Pi$ to
\[
\max_{\Pi}\; \mathrm{Acc}(\Pi)
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\mathrm{LiRA}(\Pi)\le \tau,
\quad
\mathrm{ExactFrac}(\Pi)\ge c,
\]
where $\mathrm{ExactFrac}\equiv\mathrm{RawExactFrac}=\Pr_v[\tilde p_v=\hat p_v]$ (fraction of nodes released as the unmodified posterior), $\mathrm{ReplayFrac}=\Pr_v[\tilde p_v^{(1)}=\tilde p_v^{(2)}]$ is reported separately, $\tau$ is an \emph{operator-chosen} audit risk tolerance matched to the LiRA budget (not a universal privacy target), and $c\in[0,1]$ is an operator-chosen clean fraction (study default $0.60$).
Under release-only selective Laplace with weak${=}0$, ExactFrac${=}1-\mathrm{Frac}$ by construction and implies ReplayFrac on the clean slice without a response ledger.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Uniform and stateful-noisy LBP under $c{>}0$]
\label{rem:uniform-infeasible}
Independent Laplace noise with scale $b{>}0$ on every coordinate changes $\hat p_v$ almost surely, so ExactFrac$=0$ for any uniform fresh LBP policy.
A global per-node response cache or a deterministic PRNG keyed by (node, epoch) can raise ReplayFrac to $1$ and restore cache hits / ledger audit, but ExactFrac remains $0$ (Table~\ref{tab:sla}).
Selective release with protected fraction Frac achieves ExactFrac$=1-\mathrm{Frac}$ by construction, without storing non-raw scores.
\end{remark}
This reframes the Acc--LiRA comparison.
Equal-mass LBP can win Acc subject to LiRA$\le\tau$ when $c{=}0$ is allowed; HARP solves the strictly harder problem with ExactFrac$\ge c{>}0$.
The Frac sweep traces HARP's Pareto frontier under that constraint.

\subsection{Threat model}
The adversary is a black-box client: it queries softmax scores and tries to guess whether a node was in the training set~\cite{salem2019mlleaks,olatunji2021mia,carlini2022membership}.
It does not see gradients or embeddings.
White-box parameter access remains out of scope; label-only gap AUROC and edge-similarity membership AUROC are reported alongside LiRA (GAP-agg cuts edge AUROC $0.895\rightarrow 0.748$ on Cora).

We also stress three practical variants: defense-aware shadows, multi-query averaging ($K\in\{1,5,20\}$), and an LTE-aware query adversary that focuses on clean nodes, protected-set boundaries, and top-decile LTE seeds (Sec.~\ref{sec:adaptive}).
We do not claim robustness to poisoning or unbounded queries without a session budget.

\subsection{Metrics}
\textbf{Utility:} test Acc (primary).
\textbf{Audit:} LiRA AUROC~\cite{carlini2022membership} and TPR@1\% FPR, plus Salem-style $\phi$/confidence attacks~\cite{salem2019mlleaks}.
\textbf{Fidelity:} ECE~\cite{guo2017calibration}, clean-slice ECE, ExactFrac.
LiRA decides \emph{whether} to spend noise; Acc/ECE measure the cost.

\begin{remark}
The primary protocol is transductive (non-members remain in the graph during training), matching common semi-supervised GNN APIs.
We additionally report an inductive edge-exclusion check on Cora (Sec.~\ref{sec:inductive}) where test-incident edges are dropped at train/infer time.
\end{remark}

\section{HARP: Hop-Aware Selective Release}
\label{sec:harp}

\begin{figure}[!t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.98\linewidth]{fig_harp_schematic}
\caption{HARP pipeline: risk ranking $\rightarrow$ high-risk seeds $\rightarrow$ $k$-hop expansion $\rightarrow$ protector only on the protected set (ExactFrac${=}1-\mathrm{Frac}$ on the clean majority).}
\label{fig:harp}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Design principle}
Uniform \emph{fresh} posterior noise cannot meet ExactFrac$\ge c{>}0$---any independent $b{>}0$ draw changes $\hat p_v$.
Stateful wrappers (global caches, ledgers, seeded PRNGs) can meet ReplayFrac without ExactFrac (Table~\ref{tab:sla}).
Node-local selection without hop coverage is incomplete for GNNs: if $v$ is protected but neighbor $u$ is released exact, aggregation can reintroduce membership signal into $u$'s score.
HARP is a three-interface serving framework (Fig.~\ref{fig:harp}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Constructor (pluggable):} rank nodes---topology (LTE), degree, train-neighbor exposure, confidence/entropy, random, or audit-guided vulnerability.
  \item \textbf{Geometry + protector:} expand $\mathcal{P}_k=\mathrm{Ball}_k(\mathcal{R})$; apply Laplace or masking only on $\mathcal{P}_k$.
  \item \textbf{CFS + dials:} validation-time search of Frac under ExactFrac$\ge c$ and optional LiRA$\le\tau$; log Mass/Frac/\(B\).
\end{enumerate}
Locked study config: \emph{release-only} Laplace, Frac${=}0.40$ (ExactFrac${=}0.60$), $k{=}1$, $\sigma{=}0.30$.
Topology LTE is the no-shadow default constructor; ensemble/audit-guided ranking is preferred when shadows are already paid for.
Train-time alignment is an optional ablation---not required for the ExactFrac claim.

\paragraph{Noise mass and clean fraction.}
$\mathrm{Mass}=\sum_v\sigma_v$ and $\mathrm{Frac}=|\mathcal{P}_k|/n$ are operator accounting metrics.
Uniform LBP at scale $b$ has Mass${=}bn$ and Frac${=}1$.
See paragraph~\ref{para:mass-identity} for the locked-budget identity.

\paragraph{Locked-config example (Cora).}
On Cora ($n{=}2708$), Frac${=}0.40$ protects ${\approx}1083$ nodes after one-hop expansion; ${\approx}1625$ nodes stay exact.
Mass$_{\mathrm{HARP}}{\approx}325$ matches equal-mass LBP with $b{=}0.12$.
Strong LBP ($b{=}0.3$) spends Mass${\approx}812$ on all nodes---the Acc-collapse baseline in Table~\ref{tab:fair}.

\paragraph{Release transform (Laplace protector).}
Let $\sigma_v=\sigma_{\mathrm{strong}}\mathbbm{1}[v\in\mathcal{P}_k]$.
The released posterior is
\begin{equation}
\tilde p_v=\mathrm{renorm}\big(\big[\hat p_v+\mathrm{Lap}(0,\sigma_v)\big]_+\big),
\label{eq:harp-release}
\end{equation}
with independent Laplace coordinates and the same transform on every defense-aware shadow.
The masking protector instead returns $\hat p_v$ for $v\notin\mathcal{P}_k$ and a one-hot of $\arg\max\hat p_v$ for $v\in\mathcal{P}_k$.

\paragraph{Algorithm (systems view).}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Risk:} $r\leftarrow\mathrm{Constructor}(G,\mathcal{T})$ (topology / degree / confidence / random / audit).
  \item \textbf{CFS (optional):} search Frac$\le 1-c$ to maximize Acc subject to LiRA$\le\tau$.
  \item \textbf{Seeds + expand:} binary-search seeds so $|\mathrm{Ball}_k(\mathcal{R})|/n\approx\mathrm{Frac}$; $\mathcal{P}_k\leftarrow\mathrm{Ball}_k(\mathcal{R})$.
  \item \textbf{Release:} apply~\eqref{eq:harp-release} (or masking); log $(\mathrm{Mass},\mathrm{Frac},\mathrm{Acc},\mathrm{LiRA},\mathrm{ECE})$.
\end{enumerate}
Train-time alignment is optional and omitted in the locked release-only configuration.

\subsection{Risk constructors}
\label{sec:lte}
The constructor interface is part of the framework contribution.
\textbf{Topology (LTE)} uses only $G$, $y|_{\mathcal{T}}$, and the train mask (inverse degree, local heterophily, train-neighbor exposure); it is a cheap default when shadows are unavailable---not claimed to be privacy-optimal.
\textbf{Degree / train-neighbor} isolate individual topology cues.
\textbf{Confidence / entropy} rank from a clean forward pass (high max-confidence or high entropy).
\textbf{Random} is the matched-Frac selectivity control.
\textbf{Audit-guided} scores $|\mu_{\mathrm{IN}}-\mu_{\mathrm{OUT}}|$ over a small shadow ensemble~\cite{aerni2024misleading} and is preferred when that ensemble is already paid for.
We report Spearman correlation between each constructor and audit vulnerability, plus Acc/LiRA/slice AUROC at matched Frac (Table~\ref{tab:constructors}).
On Cora/Citeseer, \emph{entropy} is the strongest cheap prior (Spearman ${\approx}0.33$--$0.35$); topology (LTE) is moderate (${\approx}0.25$--$0.28$); max-confidence is anti-correlated.
Heterophilic Chameleon priors are weak---selectivity still enforces ExactFrac, but ranking gains are limited.
Where topology underperforms random on LiRA (e.g., Cora in Table~\ref{tab:constructors}), we state that plainly: ExactFrac comes from selectivity, not from LTE.

\subsection{Why $k$-hop expansion}
\label{sec:hop}
Let $\mathcal{R}$ be seeds.
A GNN score at $u$ already mixes information from $\mathrm{Ball}_k(u)$ before any release noise is applied.
If a membership-sensitive seed $v\in\mathcal{R}$ is noised at release but a neighbor $u$ is returned bit-exact, the adversary simply queries $u$.

\begin{proposition}[Hop coverage under a one-hop linear mixer]
\label{thm:hop-necessary}
Fix a one-hop score model $s_u = \alpha\, z_v + (1-\alpha)\, z_u$ for $u\in\mathcal{N}(v)$, $\alpha\in(0,1]$, where $z_v$ carries a membership cue of gap $\gamma>0$, and release adds independent Laplace noise only to chosen nodes' scores.
If the policy noises $v$ but leaves $u$ exact, an adversary observing $s_u$ retains the unprotected leaf advantage.
Noising $\mathrm{Ball}_1(\{v\})$ places Laplace noise on every score that linearly depends on $z_v$ under this mixer.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Under seed-only protection the released leaf equals the clean mixer output, so the member/non-member gap in $\mathbb{E}[s_u]$ remains $\alpha\gamma$.
Expanding protection to $u$ subjects that coordinate to Lap$(0,\sigma)$.
A synthetic mixer confirms the ordering: unprotected and seed-only leaf AUROCs stay near $1.0$, while hop-$1$ protection lowers the leaf attack to ${\approx}0.72$.
\end{proof}
This is a motivating linearized argument for hop expansion, not a necessity theorem for nonlinear multi-layer GNNs.
Empirically we set $k$ to the model's receptive field (default $k{=}1$ for 2-layer SAGE/GCN; $k{\in}\{0,2\}$ changes Acc by ${\lesssim}0.01$ at matched Frac).
Frac---not $k$---is the population Acc--Mass knob.

\subsection{Guarantees: CFS configuration and DP composition}
\label{sec:guarantees}
\begin{remark}[CFS is validation-time configuration, not a novel optimum]
\label{thm:cfs-opt}
Constrained Frac Search (CFS) enumerates a finite grid $\mathcal{F}\subseteq[0,1-c]$ and returns $f^\star=\arg\max\{\mathrm{Acc}(f_i):\mathrm{LiRA}(f_i)\le\tau\}$ using \emph{validation} Acc and a validation-time defense-aware LiRA estimate; the locked tables then report a held-out test Acc / headline LiRA once.
This is a reproducible operator procedure for choosing Frac under explicit $(c,\tau)$---not a claim of global optimality over all release policies or constructors.
The audit cap $\tau$ is an operator risk tolerance tied to the attack budget (study headline: $n_{\mathrm{sh}}{=}16$, $\tau{=}0.70$); $\tau{=}0.55$ is infeasible at that budget, and we report neighboring caps $\{0.68,0.70,0.75\}$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:harp-results}).
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Post-processing composition]
\label{thm:compose}
Let $T$ be any training procedure whose released model satisfies $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP w.r.t.\ the training data, and let $\Pi$ be a HARP release policy that is a (possibly randomized) function only of the model's released outputs and \emph{public} deployment metadata.
Then HARP$\circ T$ satisfies the same $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP (standard post-processing~\cite{dwork2006dp}) and attains ExactFrac${=}1-\mathrm{Frac}$ w.r.t.\ the DP model's posteriors.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Immediate from the post-processing property whenever $\Pi$ does not re-read private training labels or private membership bits beyond what $T$ already released.
\end{proof}
Prop.~\ref{thm:compose} is not a new DP theorem; it is the reason HARP can sit \emph{above} a DP trainer as a serving layer.
\textbf{Constructor caveat:} topology LTE uses the train mask and $y|_{\mathcal{T}}$ as server-side deployment metadata.
If those bits are private under the DP threat model, use a public-metadata constructor (degree, confidence/entropy from the released DP outputs, random, or audit scores computed only from released shadow outputs) before claiming Prop.~\ref{thm:compose}.
Section~\ref{sec:harp-results} measures HARP$\circ$GAP under the public-metadata reading.

\subsection{Complexity and accounting}
\label{sec:complexity}
\begin{proposition}[HARP setup and release complexity]
\label{thm:harp-complexity}
Let $G=(V,E)$ have $n=|V|$ nodes and $m=|E|$ adjacency endpoints, with $C$ classes.
Fix hop radius $k=O(1)$ and $T=O(1)$ binary-search iterations for a target protected fraction.
Then setup (LTE + seeds + $k$-hop expand) runs in $O(m + n\log n)$ time and $O(n+m)$ space.
Release runs in $O(nC)$---matching uniform LBP's tier, with less noise-sampling work whenever $\mathrm{Frac}<1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
LTE scans edges then normalizes $n$ scores: $O(m+n)$.
Seed selection is $O(n\log n)$ plus $T$ BFS expansions at $O(m+n)$.
Release writes scales, samples Laplace on $\mathcal{P}_k$, and renormalizes: $O(nC)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Per-response Laplace; no global DP claim]
\label{thm:local-eps}
Interpret each node's clean posterior as a query with $\ell_1$-sensitivity at most $\Delta{=}2$ on the simplex.
Independent Lap$(\sigma)$ on coordinates of $v\in\mathcal{P}_k$ is a per-response Laplace mechanism with scale $\sigma$; unprotected nodes receive no noise.
Any policy with ExactFrac$>0$ has infinite \emph{global} $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP by construction.
We report this only as an accounting remark---not as a local-DP guarantee under graph adjacency---and recommend training-time DP (e.g., GAP) when a legal global $\varepsilon$ is required~\cite{sajadmanesh2023gap}.
\end{remark}
\paragraph{Accounting identity (stated once).}
\label{para:mass-identity}
Under locked Frac and fixed $\sigma_{\mathrm{strong}}$, Mass$_{\mathrm{HARP}}=\mathrm{Frac}\cdot n\cdot\sigma_{\mathrm{strong}}$ by construction.
Mass is an operational dial correlated with mean $\ell_1$ distortion and top-1 flip rate (at matched Mass $325$ on Cora: HARP mean $\ell_1$ $0.349$ vs.\ LBP $0.493$); it is not a standard privacy budget.
Empirical claims are (i)~feasibility under ExactFrac$\ge c$, (ii)~Acc/ECE within the selective class at matched Frac/Mass, and (iii)~serving telemetry.

\subsection{What HARP is not}
HARP is not differential privacy and is not a claim of lower population LiRA than uniform noise under ExactFrac$=0$.
It is not a claim that randomization ``breaks caching''---stateful caches and seeded noise can stabilize non-raw responses.
It is a constrained ExactFrac serving framework: spend a protector on a hop-expanded subset, keep a raw-posterior majority, configure Frac with CFS, and audit residual risk---including on unprotected nodes.


\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\subsection{Pre-declared rules}
\textbf{Primary objective:} Acc under ExactFrac$\ge c$ (study default $c{=}0.60$; swept in Fig.~\ref{fig:exactfrac-pareto}).
\textbf{Primary metrics:} Acc, ExactFrac, ECE (fidelity).
\textbf{Audit metric:} LiRA AUROC (and clean-slice confidence AUROC) as deployment constraints---not the headline win condition.
\textbf{Operational metrics:} Mass, Frac, ExactFrac (RawExactFrac), ReplayFrac, shared-cache hit rate (Table~\ref{tab:sla}).
Hyperparameters are not selected to minimize test LiRA.
Under $c{>}0$, primary baselines are selective policies at matched Frac; equal-mass LBP is the unconstrained ($c{=}0$) reference; sticky/global-cache/seeded LBP are ReplayFrac-positive but ExactFrac-infeasible controls.

\subsection{Systems metrics (definitions)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{ExactFrac} ($\equiv$RawExactFrac): fraction of nodes with $\tilde p_v=\hat p_v$.
  \item \textbf{ReplayFrac}: fraction of nodes with equal responses under re-query (may use cache/seed state).
  \item \textbf{Mass} $=\sum_v \sigma_v$: operational Laplace-scale spend (correlated with distortion).
  \item \textbf{Frac} $=|\mathcal{P}_k|/n$: protected fraction (ExactFrac${=}1-\mathrm{Frac}$ under weak${=}0$).
  \item \textbf{ECE}: calibration of released probabilities.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Locked HARP (release-only)}
Release-only selective Laplace: $\lambda{=}0$, no HCAG, no train-on-protected; $k{=}1$, $\sigma_{\mathrm{strong}}{=}0.30$, Frac${=}0.40$ (ExactFrac${=}0.60$).
Constructor default in locked tables: topology (LTE); audit/random/degree/confidence appear in Table~\ref{tab:constructors}.
Train-time alignment is reported only as an ablation.
Baselines: equal-mass LBP $b{=}0.12$; strong LBP $b{=}0.3$; MemGuard; GTD; MaskArmor; selective masking; DP-SGD Acc-tuned (vacuous $\varepsilon$).

\subsection{Baselines and attacks}
Gaussian LiRA with defense-aware shadows: $n_{\mathrm{shadows}}{=}16$ for the headline Cora table and CFS (Tables~\ref{tab:fair},~\ref{tab:cfs}; $\tau{=}0.70$), $n_{\mathrm{shadows}}{=}4$ for multi-dataset grids and Acc dials, and sweeps $\{4,16,64\}$ on Cora/Chameleon.
Five seeds with mean$\pm$95\% CI on primary Cora/Chameleon/Citeseer constructor tables; paired seeds across defenses.
ogbn-arxiv: NeighborLoader serving Acc with LiRA at $n_{\mathrm{shadows}}{\in}\{2,4,16\}$ (audit stress, not a million-node privacy claim).
ogbn-products: full-graph serving RSS/QPS; BFS LiRA ladder at $n{\in}\{15\text{k},40\text{k}\}$, $n_{\mathrm{sh}}{\in}\{2,4,8\}$, plus a canary-stressed $15$k probe with undefended canary-LiRA ${\approx}1.0$.

\subsection{What each baseline tests}
\textbf{Equal-mass LBP} ($b{=}0.12$) matches HARP Mass at Frac${=}0.40$ and is the unconstrained ($c{=}0$) reference.
\textbf{Selective constructors} (random/degree/confidence/entropy/audit/topology) isolate ranking quality at matched Frac under the SLA.
\textbf{Strong LBP} ($b{=}0.3$) is a secondary published recipe that collapses Acc under full-graph strong noise.
\textbf{MemGuard} (reimplementation) fits a standardized MLP $\phi$-attack on holdout members/non-members and searches an $L_1$ ball to drive $P(\mathrm{member})\rightarrow 0.5$ while preserving argmax (ExactFrac${=}0$).
Following Aerni et al.~\cite{aerni2024misleading} we report both $\phi$ and LiRA; at $n_{\mathrm{sh}}{=}16$ our port cuts LiRA vs.\ undefended ($0.813\rightarrow 0.667$) while remaining ExactFrac-infeasible.
\textbf{GAP-agg} is a GAP-inspired aggregation-Laplace baseline: noise is train-only, so eval ExactFrac${=}1$ with analytical $(\varepsilon,\delta)$ (hybrid DP-train + ExactFrac-serve). Not a full GAP codebase reproduction.
\textbf{Selective masking} is an alternate protector (ExactFrac preserved; often worse LiRA).
\textbf{HARP-full} (Frac${=}1$) is the unconstrained ablation that recovers strong-LBP Acc--LiRA.
\textbf{GTD}/\textbf{MaskArmor} are train-time defenses (no release Mass/Frac).
\textbf{Clip{+}noise DP-SGD} remains an Acc-tuned vacuous-$\varepsilon$ reference.

\subsection{Attack protocol (summary)}
We train defense-aware shadow models, release transformed posteriors, and report LiRA AUROC with $\phi$-features~\cite{carlini2022membership,salem2019mlleaks}.
Validation nodes are excluded from membership labels; test Acc/ECE use the held-out test split.
Multi-query experiments average $K\in\{1,5,20\}$ independent draws; session policies cap fresh draws at $B{=}5$.

\paragraph{Negative controls.}
PubMed (near-chance undefended LiRA): HARP still preserves Acc vs.\ strong LBP at Frac${=}0.40$ ($0.839$ vs.\ $0.768$); ogbn-arxiv/products give near-chance or memory-bounded audits under matched budgets---audit first, harden second~\cite{hu2020ogb}.

\subsection{Overview}
\paragraph{Operational headline (constraint-first).}
Under ExactFrac$\ge 0.60$: (i)~HARP is feasible and uniform/stateful-noisy LBP are not (Table~\ref{tab:feas});

(iii)~cache microbenchmarks show hit-rate gains when many responses are bit-stable (Table~\ref{tab:serving});
(iv)~equal-mass LBP can still win Acc--LiRA when $c{=}0$ is allowed---that unconstrained privacy-only regime is out of scope for the SLA.

\paragraph{Reading the tables.}
Compare policies only inside the feasible ExactFrac set for your $c$.
Acc is the primary utility number; LiRA/TPR@1\% FPR tell the operator whether to raise Frac or tighten $\tau$; ECE reports score quality on the released (especially clean) majority.
Questions: \textbf{(Q1)} feasibility under $c{>}0$; \textbf{(Q2)} Acc/ECE vs.\ selective and equal-mass baselines; \textbf{(Q3)} constructors vs.\ random on utility \emph{and} slice privacy; \textbf{(Q4)} serving cost and clean-slice residual risk; \textbf{(Q5)} OGB volume Acc/serving with scoped audits.

\paragraph{HARP$\circ$GAP: DP trainer as a component.}

\paragraph{Exact scores $\neq$ membership safety.}
Clean nodes are well calibrated (ECE$_{\mathrm{clean}}{=}0.018$), but they are not automatically hard to attack.

\paragraph{Velocity: multi-query and session budgets.}
Averaging $K$ independent releases denoises Laplace and moves toward the undefended baseline (Cora 5-seed LiRA at $K{=}1/5/20$: HARP $0.536/0.546/0.551$; $n_{\mathrm{sh}}{=}4$).

\textbf{Enforcement assumption:} server-side state bound to authenticated identities with rate limits.

Deploy shared $B$ (or authenticated single-principal sessions) when Sybil velocity is in scope; Frac and $B$ remain complementary controls.

\paragraph{Why ExactFrac matters (RawExactFrac $\neq$ ReplayFrac).}
Caches and ledgers can stabilize previously sampled noisy outputs~\cite{crankshaw2017clipper,triton2024cache}; that is ReplayFrac, not ExactFrac.
Table~\ref{tab:sla} (Cora; 3 seeds; 8 clients; $20$k Zipf; shared LRU $1024$) makes the separation concrete: fresh LBP has both fractions ${\approx}0$; per-client sticky LBP raises within-client ReplayFrac but not cross-client equality or ExactFrac; \emph{global} first-draw caches and deterministic seeded LBP achieve ReplayFrac${=}1$ and high cache hits with ExactFrac${=}0$; HARP alone among noisy policies meets ExactFrac${=}0.60$ without storing non-raw scores.
Table~\ref{tab:cache} confirms clean-slice bit-stability; Table~\ref{tab:serving} is an \emph{in-process response-cache microbenchmark} (not end-to-end networked API throughput; absolute QPS/latency are mechanism artifacts on Cora).

\paragraph{Clean-slice residual risk (first-class) and the mitigation stack.}
\label{sec:adaptive}
Unprotected nodes are \emph{not} claimed safe (Table~\ref{tab:adaptive}): clean-slice conf AUROC rises to $0.63$--$0.67$ while population LiRA stays ${\approx}0.53$.
HARP attacks this from three composable directions (all at ExactFrac${=}0.60$, 5 seeds):
(i)~\emph{constructor}: ensemble ranking removes vulnerable members from the exact slice, $0.669\rightarrow 0.594$ (Table~\ref{tab:constructors});
(ii)~\emph{deterministic confidence smoothing (DCS)}: released rows on the clean slice with $p_{\max}{>}\theta$ are flattened by a fixed temperature ($\theta{=}0.9$, $T{=}3$)---a deterministic, argmax-preserving post-map, so re-queries stay bitwise stable, caches and audit replay still work, and routing decisions are unchanged; slice AUROC falls $0.650\rightarrow 0.618$ under topology ranking and $0.591\rightarrow 0.576$ on top of ensemble, with \emph{zero} Acc change;
(iii)~\emph{slice-aware CFS}: adds a slice-AUROC cap to the search.
DCS trades raw-score fidelity on smoothed rows for slice privacy while preserving ReplayFrac and threshold stability; when ExactFrac (raw $\hat p_v$) is contractual, skip DCS.
The stacked floor (${\approx}0.575$) is structural at $c{=}0.60$: a $60\%$ deterministic slice must retain some membership signal.
Lower $c$, or use GAP-agg / HARP$\circ$GAP, if tighter slice control is required.

\paragraph{Operator workflow.}
Audit with defense-aware LiRA ($n_{\mathrm{sh}}{\ge}16$ when affordable); if LiRA${\approx}0.5$ set Frac${=}0$; else CFS on val Acc under $(c,\tau)$ (Table~\ref{tab:frac}); prefer ensemble constructors + optional DCS for slice risk; enforce shared $B{=}1$ under Sybil velocity; use HARP$\circ$GAP when a legal $\varepsilon$ is required.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
HARP is a Big-Data score-serving mechanism for ExactFrac$\ge c{>}0$---not a claim to beat uniform noise when $c{=}0$, and not an equation of ReplayFrac with raw-posterior equality.
The venue fit is prediction-API infrastructure under Volume/Velocity/Veracity: selective release, cache/replay accounting, and audits on high-volume graphs---membership privacy enters as an operator dial on that serving stack.
ExactFrac formalizes a raw-score SLA that generic response caches do not automatically provide~\cite{crankshaw2017clipper,triton2024cache}; $c{=}0.60$ is a study default, and sticky/global/seeded noise are not substitutes (Table~\ref{tab:sla}).
GAP-agg already meets ExactFrac${=}1$ with finite (often large) $\varepsilon$; HARP's complementary claim is high-Acc ExactFrac without training DP, plus constructors/CFS/slice controls (Prop.~\ref{thm:compose}).
\textbf{Limits:} (i)~ExactFrac${>}0$ $\Rightarrow$ infinite global $\varepsilon$ on released scores; (ii)~clean-slice AUROC stays above chance at $c{=}0.60$---structural; (iii)~CFS $\tau$ is an operator dial that must track the LiRA budget; (iv)~products privacy is a BFS/canary ladder; (v)~GAP-agg/MemGuard are reimplementations; (vi)~serving benches are in-process microbenchmarks, not production traces.
Future: learned constructors, distributed full-graph LiRA, production API traces.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
HARP equips GNN prediction APIs with ExactFrac-constrained score serving: selective release meets raw-posterior $c{>}0$ where fresh and stateful-noisy uniform policies cannot, constructors cut clean-slice attack AUROC, CFS configures Frac under operator $(c,\tau)$, and HARP$\circ$GAP preserves training DP for public-metadata constructors.
Headline evidence uses $n_{\mathrm{sh}}{=}16$; residual clean-slice risk and scoped large-graph Acc/serving probes are reported honestly.
Use training-time DP (alone or under HARP) when a legal global $\varepsilon$ is required.
